# Best Family Restaurants in Orlando: Tried, Tested & Totally Worth It

Planning a family trip to Orlando? You already know the theme parks are incredible — but what about the meals in between? Choosing the right restaurant can make or break a family day out, especially when you have little ones (and hungry adults) to please. The good news: Orlando's dining scene is just as impressive as its attractions.

We've done the legwork so you don't have to. Here are five family restaurants in Orlando that genuinely deliver — fun atmosphere, crowd-pleasing menus, and memories that last well beyond dessert.

---

## 1. T-Rex Café — Dine Inside a Prehistoric Adventure

**📍 1676 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL (Disney Springs)**

If your kids are obsessed with dinosaurs — or even if they're not — T-Rex Café is an absolute must. Located at Disney Springs, this immersive restaurant fills its dining room with life-size animatronic dinosaurs, erupting volcanoes, and simulated meteor showers that kick in every few minutes. Yes, you're basically eating inside a theme park attraction.

The menu is broad enough for everyone: burgers, pasta, seafood, and a solid kids' menu. There's also a fossil dig area and a gift shop packed with dino merchandise for the little ones.

### Why families love it
- Spectacular theming that keeps kids entertained between bites
- Menu options for picky eaters and adventurous adults alike
- Right inside Disney Springs — no park ticket required

**Best for:** Families with young children and dinosaur fans of all ages

---

## 2. Café Tu Tu Tango — Where Dinner Feels Like an Art Show

**📍 8625 International Dr, Orlando, FL**

This one is a hidden gem that locals absolutely swear by. Inspired by the artist studios of Barcelona, Café Tu Tu Tango serves tapas-style small plates meant for sharing — which makes dinner interactive, fun, and surprisingly adventurous for kids. The eclectic menu draws from global flavors, with everything from crispy calamari and flatbreads to more creative dishes that change regularly.

What really sets this place apart is the atmosphere: paintings cover every wall, sculptures are scattered throughout, and live artists work on canvases right in the dining room while you eat. It's dinner and a show.

### Why families love it
- Sharing plates mean everyone gets to try a little of everything
- No rigid ordering — great for indecisive families
- The live art element sparks conversations and keeps curious kids engaged

**Best for:** Families who like to explore new flavors and a bit of culture with their meal

---

## 3. The Cowfish Sushi Burger Bar — Unlikely Combo, Unforgettable Meal

**📍 6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL (Universal CityWalk)**

Sushi and burgers in the same restaurant? It sounds like it shouldn't work — until you sit down and realize it absolutely does. The Cowfish at Universal CityWalk has built its reputation on the "Burgushi," a creative fusion of burger and sushi that you simply have to try to believe. But if someone in your group isn't feeling adventurous, the traditional burger and sushi menus are just as strong.

This is the kind of restaurant where every family member finds exactly what they want, no compromises needed. And before you leave — do not skip the milkshakes. They are ridiculously good.

### Why families love it
- Something for everyone, from sushi lovers to burger purists
- The Burgushi concept is genuinely fun and social media-worthy
- Milkshakes that will be talked about for the rest of the trip

**Best for:** Families with mixed tastes and teens who appreciate something a little different

---

## 4. Sicilian Oven — Proper Italian, No Fuss

For those moments when you just want real, comforting food after a long park day, Sicilian Oven delivers. Wood-fired pizzas, handmade pastas, generous portions — this is Italian cooking done with care. The atmosphere is warm and relaxed, the staff are attentive with children, and the prices are reasonable compared to many tourist-area spots.

It's a great choice when the family needs a slower pace and a proper sit-down meal without the theatrical bells and whistles.

### Why families love it
- Authentic wood-fired pizza that rivals what you'd find in Italy
- Quiet, welcoming environment — perfect after a hectic park day
- Portions are generous enough to share

**Best for:** Families craving classic Italian comfort food

---

## 5. Rainforest Café — A Jungle Adventure at the Table

**📍 Available at Disney Springs and Disney's Animal Kingdom**

A true Orlando classic — and for good reason. Rainforest Café immerses guests in a lush tropical jungle complete with animatronic animals, bubbling aquariums, and dramatic thunderstorm effects that roll through the dining room periodically. For young children especially, the experience is absolutely magical before a single dish arrives.

The menu covers all the classics: burgers, grilled chicken, pasta, salads, and a dedicated kids' menu. Having two locations — Disney Springs and Animal Kingdom — makes it easy to fit into almost any itinerary.

**Best for:** Families with young children who love animals and immersive environments
