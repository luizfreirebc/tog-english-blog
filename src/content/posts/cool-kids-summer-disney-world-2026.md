Summer at Disney World is a full experience — and 2026's **Cool Kids Summer** programming adds enough new content to make it genuinely worth the trip, even if you've been before. But not all of it is created equal. Here's our honest breakdown.

## What's New for Summer 2026

### Magic Kingdom
The **Tomorrowland Summer Bash** is back with new light installations and an updated DJ set running nightly. The crowd energy here is real — kids go absolutely wild, and the effects are genuinely impressive against the castle backdrop.

The Enchanted Tiki Room also received a seasonal overlay with a tropical summer soundtrack. Light programming change, but fun for younger kids.

### EPCOT
**Epcot International Food & Wine Festival** starts earlier this year — late June rather than August — meaning summer visitors now get access to both the Cool Kids events and the full Food & Wine experience simultaneously.

The new **EPCOT Energy Exchange** exhibit in Innoventions-adjacent space is geared toward 8-12 year olds and surprisingly engaging. It's a free walk-through, low lines, and genuinely educational.

### Hollywood Studios
No major new summer overlay here, but the **Jedi Training: Trials of the Temple** runs multiple times daily in summer, and it's still one of the best experiences for kids aged 4-12. Book it as soon as you arrive — spots fill up within the first hour.

## What's NOT Worth the Hype

- **Cool Kids Summer Photo Opportunities**: The themed photo spots have long waits (30-45 min) for a single pic. The free Disney PhotoPass snaps at classic spots are faster and better.
- **The Seasonal Merchandise**: Cute, but aggressively priced. The same Magic Kingdom logo on summer-colored items at a premium. Skip unless your kids have their heart set on it.

## The Bottom Line

If you're going with kids aged 5-14, **EPCOT + Magic Kingdom** in a single trip is the strongest combo this summer. Hollywood Studios earns its ticket price regardless of the seasonal theming. Animal Kingdom is excellent year-round but doesn't add much specifically for Cool Kids Summer.
