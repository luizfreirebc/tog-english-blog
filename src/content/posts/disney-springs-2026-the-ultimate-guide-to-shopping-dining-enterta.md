If you're visiting Orlando and think Walt Disney World is all about theme park tickets and early morning rope drops — think again. **Disney Springs** is one of the best free experiences in the entire resort, and in 2026, it's better than ever. Whether you're a first-timer or a returning guest, this guide covers everything you need to know before you go.

## What Is Disney Springs?

Formerly known as Downtown Disney, Disney Springs is the shopping, dining, and entertainment district of Walt Disney World Resort. It sits alongside a beautiful lakefront and draws inspiration from Florida's coastal towns and natural spring landscapes — you'll notice that the whole complex is connected by a scenic waterway, and the iconic water tower at the entrance has become something of a landmark in its own right.

The best part? **There's no admission fee.** You walk in, you explore, you eat, you shop — all without a theme park ticket.

Today, Disney Springs has more than **150 shops, restaurants, and experiences**, which is roughly double what it offered in its Downtown Disney days. It's divided into four distinct neighborhoods, each with its own personality.

## The Four Neighborhoods of Disney Springs

### Town Center

This is the newest section of Disney Springs, with its first stores opening in May 2016. Town Center has a more polished, upscale feel — think sophisticated architecture, open-air plazas, and a mix of well-known brands alongside unique boutiques. It's a great starting point if you're visiting for the first time.

### The Landing

If food is your priority (and honestly, it should be), The Landing is where you want to spend your time. This waterfront district is packed with standout dining options and gorgeous views over the lake. Grab a table with a waterside view and settle in — this neighborhood rewards a slow, leisurely visit.

### The Marketplace

The most family-friendly of the four neighborhoods, The Marketplace combines classic Disney magic with newer additions like a pedestrian bridge over the water. The **World of Disney** store — one of the largest Disney merchandise shops in the world — is located here, and the **LEGO Imagination Center** is a hit with kids of all ages. You'll also find the charming **Art of Disney** gallery if you're looking for something more collectible.

### West Side

West Side brings the energy. With entertainment venues, elevated walkways offering panoramic views, and a lively atmosphere especially in the evenings, this is the place to be after dark. Entertainment options include **Splitsville Luxury Lanes**, a retro-chic bowling alley with surprisingly good food, and **Aerophile**, a giant tethered helium balloon that lifts you 400 feet into the air for sweeping views of Walt Disney World — truly one of Orlando's most unique experiences.

## Practical Information for Your Visit

### Hours of Operation

Disney Springs is generally open **daily from 10:00 AM to midnight**, though individual shop and restaurant hours may vary. Dining spots tend to stay busy well into the evening, so arriving by late afternoon gives you time to explore and still enjoy a proper dinner without feeling rushed.

### Getting There: Transportation Options

**For Disney Resort Hotel Guests:**
One of the great perks of staying on Disney property is complimentary transportation. You have two convenient options:

- **Bus:** Catch any bus marked "Disney Springs" from your resort. Service typically runs from around 8:00 AM to 2:00 AM.
- **Boat:** Guests staying at **Old Key West**, **Saratoga Springs**, or **Port Orleans** can hop on a scenic ferry service that drops you right at the waterfront. It's genuinely one of the most pleasant ways to arrive.

**Driving Your Own Car:**
Driving is straightforward, and here's the great news — **standard parking is completely free**. The Orange, Lime, and Grapefruit parking garages are all included at no charge. If you want something a little closer to the action, Preferred Parking in the Lemon and Mango lots is available for **$10**, while valet parking runs a flat **$20 plus tip**.

## Quick FAQs

**Is there an entry fee for Disney Springs?**
No — entry is 100% free for everyone, no reservation required.

**Is parking really free?**
Yes! Self-parking in the main garages is complimentary, which makes Disney Springs an exceptional value, especially for families.

**Do I need a Disney World ticket to visit?**
Absolutely not. Disney Springs is open to all guests, whether or not you're visiting the theme parks during your trip.

## Final Thoughts

Disney Springs is the kind of place that surprises people — they show up expecting a shopping mall and leave wondering why they didn't plan to spend more time there. With world-class restaurants, unique entertainment, beautiful waterfront views, and that unmistakable Disney atmosphere, it earns its place on any Orlando itinerary.
