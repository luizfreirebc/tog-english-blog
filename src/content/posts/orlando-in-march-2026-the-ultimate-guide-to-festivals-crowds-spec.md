## Why March 2026 Is One of Orlando's Biggest Months Ever

Let's be honest: March in Orlando is not for the faint of heart. It's loud, it's busy, and it's absolutely electric. Between Spring Break crowds, spectacular theme park festivals, and the ramp-up of Universal's brand-new Epic Universe, March 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most event-packed months Central Florida has seen in years.

But here's the thing — if you know *when* to show up and *where* to look, March can be an extraordinary time to visit. This guide breaks down the key events, the crowd reality, and the insider tips you need to make the most of your trip.

---

## The Crowd Reality: Spring Break Week (March 16–20)

Before we dive into the fun stuff, let's talk about the elephant in the room. **Orange County Public Schools (OCPS)** have their official Spring Break from **March 16 to 20, 2026**. That means local families flood the parks, I-4 traffic gets noticeably heavier, and wait times at major attractions can stretch well beyond what you'd normally expect.

### What to do during that week

- Hit **smaller events and festivals early in the morning**, before the crowds build up
- Save the big theme park rides for **late afternoon and evening**, once local day-trippers start heading home
- Consider using a **private transfer service** rather than shared shuttle buses — it will save you time and stress, especially during this peak window

If you have any flexibility in your travel dates, arriving before March 14 or after March 22 will give you a noticeably smoother experience.

## Universal Orlando Mardi Gras 2026: International Flavors of Carnaval

One of the most anticipated events of the season, **Universal's Mardi Gras: International Flavors of Carnaval** runs throughout the entire month of March. And the best part? It's **included with standard park admission** — no extra ticket required.

The festival brings a spectacular nightly parade and live headline concerts at the Music Plaza Stage. Here's the March 2026 lineup:

- **March 7** — Ziggy Marley
- **March 14** — Bebe Rexha
- **March 15** — Zedd
- **March 21** — Barenaked Ladies
- **March 28** — The All-American Rejects

### Pro tip for the parade

If you want a great spot for the Mardi Gras parade, position yourself near the **Horror Make-Up Show** at least **45 minutes before showtime**. The crowd builds fast, and the good viewing spots disappear quickly.

Beyond the music, the international food booths scattered across the park are genuinely impressive — think global street food with bold, unexpected flavors. It's a highlight for teenagers and food lovers alike.

## Disney Springs St. Patrick's Day Festival: Authentic Irish Magic

Skip the generic green-beer parades. The best St. Patrick's Day celebration in Orlando happens at **Raglan Road Irish Pub in Disney Springs**. The **Mighty St. Patrick's Day Festival** runs from **March 13 to 17, 2026**, bringing authentic Irish dancers and live bands flown in directly from Ireland.

### What you need to know

- **March 13–16**: Reservations are strongly recommended — this event sells out
- **March 17 (St. Patrick's Day itself)**: Walk-in only, with a **$20 cover charge per adult**
- Disney Springs has free parking, making it an easy and affordable evening out

If you're traveling with young children and want something a bit more low-key, the **Winter Park St. Patrick's Day Parade on March 7** is a wonderful local alternative — festive, family-friendly, and far less crowded.

## EPCOT International Flower & Garden Festival 2026

For families, couples, and anyone who appreciates beautiful surroundings and creative food, the **EPCOT International Flower & Garden Festival** is an absolute must. Running from February through May, March is widely considered the **sweet spot** — the Florida weather is still pleasantly mild, and the floral displays are at their peak.

### What to expect

- Massive **topiary sculptures** of beloved Disney characters throughout the park
- **Themed gardens** circling the World Showcase lagoon
- **Outdoor kitchens** serving inventive seasonal dishes at every turn
- **Garden Rocks Concert Series** on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays — also included with park admission

The Garden Rocks concerts tend to feature classic acts with serious nostalgia value. Check the EPCOT event calendar closer to your visit for the confirmed lineup and grab your spot at the America Gardens Theatre early.
