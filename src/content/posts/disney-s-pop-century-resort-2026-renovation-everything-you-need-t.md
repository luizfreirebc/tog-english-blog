# Disney's Pop Century Resort 2026 Renovation: Everything You Need to Know Before You Book

If you've been eyeing Disney's Pop Century Resort as your home base for a Walt Disney World vacation, here's some exciting news — and some important planning intel you'll want before hitting that "Book Now" button. Starting in March 2026, one of Disney World's most beloved budget-friendly resorts is undergoing its most significant transformation since it opened back in 2003. We're talking a head-to-toe refresh of the lobby, the pool bar, and the guest rooms themselves.

Whether you're a first-time visitor or a returning fan who has a soft spot for those giant yo-yos and Play-Doh cans scattered around the property, this guide breaks down exactly what's changing — and what it means for your trip.

---

## What Is Disney's Pop Century Resort?

Before we dive into the renovation details, a quick intro for those who haven't stayed here yet. Disney's Pop Century Resort is one of the most affordable on-property options at Walt Disney World, making it incredibly popular with families and budget-conscious travelers from around the world.

Opened in 2003, the resort is a colorful love letter to American pop culture, with each section of the hotel celebrating a different decade — from the fabulous 1950s all the way through the totally-rad 1990s. Think oversized bowling pins, massive Rubik's Cubes, larger-than-life bowling balls, and enough neon to light up a city block. It's fun, it's kitschy, and it's unmistakably Disney.

After more than two decades of welcoming millions of guests, it's time for a serious glow-up.

---

## The 2026 Renovation: Three Big Changes Coming

### 1. A Completely Reimagined Lobby

They say first impressions matter, and Disney clearly agrees. The lobby renovation is designed to make your arrival feel like the beginning of a true Disney experience — not just a check-in line.

The new lobby concept will feature **vibrant decade-themed artwork** spanning the 1950s through the 1990s, with murals, panels, and artistic installations that capture the music, movies, TV shows, and cultural touchstones of each era. It's going to be visually stunning and deeply nostalgic all at once.

One of the most talked-about additions? A **TV Wall with vintage-style frames** — a wall of television sets styled to reflect the design aesthetic of each decade. It's already shaping up to be one of the most Instagrammable spots in the entire resort.

Beyond the aesthetics, the new lobby will also offer **more seating and social spaces**, which is genuinely welcome news for families dragging tired feet back from a long day at Magic Kingdom. The lighting scheme is also being redesigned from scratch to feel warmer, more dynamic, and more flattering in photos at any hour of the day.

### 2. A Refreshed Petals Pool Bar

If you've ever spent an afternoon lounging by Pop Century's main pool with a frozen drink in hand, you already know that the Petals Pool Bar holds a special place in the resort's social life. Good news: it's getting an upgrade.

The renovation will bring **colorful new tiling** that modernizes the bar's look while keeping that relaxed, poolside vacation vibe. **Digital menus** are being introduced to speed up service and give guests a better visual sense of what's on offer — complete with real-time updates on specials and seasonal drinks. All the surrounding fixtures, furniture, and finishes are being refreshed too, meaning the whole area will feel noticeably newer and more polished.

For families who love spending sunny Orlando afternoons by the pool — and let's be honest, that's basically everyone — this upgrade will make those downtime moments even more enjoyable.

### 3. Guest Room Renovations

Perhaps the most anticipated part of the entire project is what's happening inside the rooms themselves. After more than 20 years of hosting guests, the accommodations are due for a meaningful refresh in both comfort and style.

While Disney hasn't released every detail yet, the renovation is expected to bring updated furnishings, refreshed décor that ties back to the resort's iconic decade themes, and modern amenities that meet the expectations of today's international travelers. Think better storage solutions, improved lighting, and a general sense that everything is fresh and well-maintained.

## What This Means for Your Trip Planning

Here's the practical part: Disney typically keeps resorts open during renovations, so you don't need to avoid Pop Century entirely if your travel dates fall during the construction window. That said, it's worth being aware that some areas may be temporarily closed or noisier than usual depending on when you visit.

**Our advice?** If you're planning a trip to Walt Disney World in 2026, check the latest updates on which sections of the resort are actively under renovation during your stay, and consider requesting a room away from the work zones when you book.

The good news is that once the dust settles, guests will be walking into one of the most refreshed and exciting value resorts on Disney property.
