# Jessie's Roundup at Magic Kingdom: Your Complete Guide to This Exciting New Experience

If you're planning a trip to Walt Disney World Resort in 2025 or 2026, there's one exciting new addition you absolutely need to know about: **Jessie's Roundup** at Magic Kingdom. This brand-new themed experience is set to delight families, Pixar fans, and anyone who has ever dreamed of stepping into the world of *Toy Story*. Read on for everything you need to plan your visit like a pro.

---

## What Is Jessie's Roundup?

Jessie's Roundup is a new interactive entertainment area coming to **Magic Kingdom**, themed around the beloved Pixar *Toy Story* franchise — and specifically centered on everyone's favorite cowgirl, Jessie. The concept draws from the classic Pixar storytelling device of feeling like you've been shrunk down to toy-size, placing guests right inside the colorful, whimsical world of Andy's room and the Wild West.

What makes this addition especially exciting is *where* it's located: **Frontierland**. This is one of Magic Kingdom's most iconic and visually rich lands, already home to fan favorites like Big Thunder Mountain Railroad and the newly reimagined Tiana's Bayou Adventure (formerly Splash Mountain). Jessie fits right in — think cowboy hats, lassos, and wide-open frontier scenery that perfectly complements her character.

---

## Why Jessie's Roundup Makes Perfect Sense for Magic Kingdom

Since Toy Story Land opened at Hollywood Studios back in 2018, families have absolutely fallen in love with the immersive Pixar toy universe. However, Magic Kingdom — the most-visited park at Walt Disney World — historically had very little *Toy Story* content. Jessie's Roundup changes that in a big way.

Jessie is a character who resonates across generations. Kids aged 3–10 in particular adore her, but she has fans of all ages. Her cowgirl aesthetic blends seamlessly with Frontierland's existing look and feel, making this feel like a natural extension of the land rather than a forced addition.

Disney has also been investing heavily in **character meet-and-greets and live entertainment** in recent years, and Jessie's Roundup is expected to prioritize exactly that — putting guests face-to-face with beloved characters in beautifully designed settings.

---

## What to Expect at Jessie's Roundup

While Disney has kept some details close to the vest, here's what you can realistically expect based on similar Disney experiences:

### Immersive Theming
Expect richly detailed scenery inspired by Andy's bedroom and the classic cowboy world of Woody and Jessie. Every corner is likely to be a photo opportunity.

### Meet-and-Greet with Jessie
This is the big one. Guests should be able to meet Jessie herself in an exclusive themed setting — perfect for photos, autographs, and magical moments your kids will talk about for years.

### Interactive Activities for Little Ones
Jessie's Roundup appears to be designed with **younger children** in mind, especially those who may not yet meet the height requirements for thrill rides. Expect games, photo stations, and hands-on activities that keep toddlers and preschoolers entertained.

### Exclusive Merchandise
No Disney experience is complete without something to bring home! Look for Jessie-themed items at nearby shops — from plush toys to apparel and collectibles.

### Themed Food and Drinks
Disney loves a good themed snack, and Jessie's Roundup will likely feature specially designed treats and beverages that tie into the cowboy theme.

## Tips for Visiting Jessie's Roundup with Your Family

**Arrive early.** New Disney experiences almost always draw large crowds, especially on weekends and during peak seasons. Head to Frontierland first thing in the morning to beat the lines.

**Check the My Disney Experience app.** Disney uses this app for virtual queues, Lightning Lane passes, and character meet-and-greet wait times. Download it before your trip and have it ready to go.

**Combine with nearby attractions.** While you're in Frontierland, make the most of your time by riding Big Thunder Mountain Railroad or visiting Tiana's Bayou Adventure — they're practically next door.

**Dress the part.** Kids (and adults!) love dressing up as their favorite characters. A Jessie costume or cowboy hat will make the experience even more memorable and lead to some amazing photos.

**Be patient with wait times.** New attractions and experiences at Disney World often have longer wait times in the first months after opening. If possible, plan your visit for a weekday in the off-season for shorter queues.
