Universal Orlando Resort's most anticipated park is finally here. **Epic Universe officially opens on May 22, 2026** — and if you've been waiting to plan your trip, this is your sign. From ticket prices to the five themed worlds you'll explore, here's everything you need to know before you book.

## What Is Epic Universe?

Epic Universe is the **fourth theme park at Universal Orlando Resort** — and the largest the company has ever built. With more than 750 acres of total land, it's bigger than Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure combined.

The park is divided into **five distinct themed worlds**, each with its own rides, restaurants, and shops:

- **Wizarding World of Harry Potter — Ministry of Magic:** Set in 1990s wizarding Paris, this world brings the French Ministry of Magic to life with immersive details and brand-new attractions.
- **How to Train Your Dragon — Isle of Berk:** Inspired by the DreamWorks franchise, this Viking island world is packed with family-friendly rides and entertainment.
- **Super Nintendo World:** The American version of the beloved Mario universe, complete with Peach's Castle and interactive wearable technology.
- **Monster-Verse:** A nod to Universal's classic monsters — Dracula, Frankenstein, the Mummy — reimagined with modern thrills.
- **Celestial Park:** The central hub of the park, featuring gardens, live entertainment, and the anchor coaster **Starfall Racers**.

Early hotel guests at select Universal resorts got access starting **May 19, 2026** — three days before the general public.

## Epic Universe Ticket Prices in 2026

Universal uses **dynamic pricing**, which means the cost of a single-day ticket changes depending on the date, season, and how far in advance you buy. Here's what to expect based on officially announced pricing:

| Ticket Type | Starting Price |
|---|---|
| 1-Day Adult (10+) | From **$109** |
| 1-Day Child (3–9) | From **$104** |
| Peak season days | Up to **$179+** per person |
| Children 0–2 | **Free** |

The lowest prices are available on slower weekdays outside of school holidays. Peak dates — including summer, spring break, and major holidays — push prices toward the higher end of the range.

**Pro tip:** Buy your tickets in advance directly through the official Universal Orlando website. Not only do you lock in the best available price, but tickets for the first months of 2026 were already on sale well before the park opened.

## Do Annual Passes Cover Epic Universe?

Yes — and this is a big deal. Universal reformulated its **Epic Pass** annual pass program specifically to include Epic Universe alongside the existing parks. Depending on the tier you choose, your pass can cover:

- Universal Studios Florida
- Islands of Adventure
- Epic Universe
- Volcano Bay (water park)

Annual passes are the best option if you're planning to spend **three or more days** at Universal parks, or if you visit Orlando more than once a year. Tiers range from the most basic (with more blockout dates) to the top-tier pass with year-round access and added perks like free parking and discounts on food and merchandise.

## Multi-Park Tickets: Are They Worth It?

If you're visiting Orlando for a full week, **multi-park tickets** are almost always the smarter buy. A 2-park or 3-park ticket lets you bounce between Universal's properties without paying separately each time.

For families planning to also visit Walt Disney World Resort, it's worth noting that the two resorts are separate — passes and tickets don't cross over. Budget accordingly if you're splitting time between both.

## How to Plan Your Visit to Epic Universe

Getting the most out of a new park takes a bit of strategy, especially during the first year when crowds are higher than usual.

- **Arrive early.** Gates open before the official park hours for on-site hotel guests. Even as a day visitor, arriving 30–45 minutes before opening gives you a head start on the most popular rides.
- **Download the Universal Orlando app.** It shows real-time wait times, lets you mobile order food, and is where you'll manage any Lightning Lane or Express Pass purchases.
- **Go on a weekday.** Tuesday and Wednesday typically have the shortest lines. Avoid weekends and major holidays if possible, especially during the first year.
- **Prioritize your must-do rides.** Each of the five worlds has a headline attraction with the longest waits. Head to your top priority first, then work your way through the park.
- **Check height requirements in advance.** Some attractions in Super Nintendo World and Ministry of Magic have minimums. Knowing before you go saves disappointment at the gate.
- **Plan your meals.** The dining in Epic Universe is a major part of the experience — each world has themed restaurants that match the atmosphere. Mobile ordering through the app cuts wait time significantly.

## What to Expect on Your First Visit

Epic Universe is designed to feel genuinely different from anything else in Orlando. The level of immersion in each world is a step up even from what Universal has done before — the streets, the sounds, the food, and the cast members all tie into the theme in ways that make you feel like you've actually stepped into another place.

**Families with younger kids** will find Isle of Berk and Super Nintendo World especially welcoming — both have rides and experiences designed for a wide range of ages. **Thrill seekers** should make a beeline for Starfall Racers in Celestial Park and whatever major coaster anchors the Monster-Verse.

Plan for **a full day minimum** — and honestly, two days if you want to experience everything without rushing. With five worlds and dozens of experiences, there's no way to do it all in a few hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Epic Universe open?
Epic Universe officially opened to the general public on **May 22, 2026**. Early access for select Universal hotel guests began on May 19, 2026.

### How much does a ticket to Epic Universe cost?
Single-day tickets start at around **$109 for adults** and **$104 for children ages 3–9** on the lowest-demand days. Prices increase on busy dates and can exceed $179 per person during peak season.

### Is Epic Universe included in Universal annual passes?
Yes. Universal redesigned its **Epic Pass** annual pass lineup to include Epic Universe. The tier you choose determines which parks are covered and how many blockout dates apply.

### How many themed worlds are in Epic Universe?
Epic Universe has **five themed worlds**: Wizarding World of Harry Potter — Ministry of Magic, How to Train Your Dragon — Isle of Berk, Super Nintendo World, Monster-Verse, and Celestial Park.

### How long do you need for Epic Universe?
Most visitors should plan for **at least one full day**. If you want to explore every world, dine at themed restaurants, and catch entertainment, two days gives you a much more relaxed experience.
